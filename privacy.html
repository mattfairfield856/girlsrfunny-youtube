Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Who we are

Girls R Funny Publisher ("the app") is operated by the Girls R Funny YouTube channel (@GirlsRFunny) for its own use. Contact: pdga889735@gmail.com.

What the app does

The channel owner connects the channel's own Google account through Google's sign-in (OAuth 2.0). The app then uploads the channel's own finished videos to that channel using the YouTube Data API (videos.insert) and checks the status of videos it uploaded.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy is at http://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

Data the app accesses

Only what the owner approves on Google's consent screen:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: read the connected channel's name and ID, to confirm the right channel was connected, and the processing and visibility status of videos the app uploaded.

The app does not access comments, subscribers, analytics, playlists, messages, or any information about other YouTube users.

How the data is used and stored

The access and refresh tokens are stored on the owner's own computer, in encrypted operating-system storage, and are used only to upload the owner's videos and read the status of those uploads. They are not sent to any other service, not shared, not sold, and not used for advertising, analytics or profiling. The app has no database of users and no server that collects data. No YouTube data is stored beyond the uploaded video's ID and status on the owner's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Girls R Funny Publisher's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

The tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the app (Disconnect), or on the Google Account permissions page, https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, and can delete the stored token. After revocation the app can no longer reach the channel.

Children

The app is not directed to children and has no users other than the channel owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
